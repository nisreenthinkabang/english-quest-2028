LEARNING DATA ATTRIBUTION

Derived subset: English headwords selected from LexiCore 1.0.0, X-Trivle (2026), CC BY 4.0, https://github.com/X-Trivle/LexiCore. Modified: Thai sense mapping, filters, ID fields. LexiCore POS labels are not used because sample checks found errors. CEFR bands are dataset suggestions, not certification.

Thai meanings: Thai WordNet, Copyright 2011 NICT, via Open Multilingual Wordnet https://github.com/omwn/omw-data/tree/main/wns/tha. English definitions/examples: Princeton WordNet 3.0. Meanings are linked by synset, not machine-translated. A card shows one selected sense; word forms are not word families.
